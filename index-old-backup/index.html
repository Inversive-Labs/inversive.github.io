Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a security audit?

+

Security audits are essential to identify vulnerabilities before deployment, protecting your users' assets and your project's reputation.

How long does an audit take?

+

Audit duration varies based on codebase complexity, typically ranging from 2-6 weeks for most projects.

What does the audit process involve?

+

Our process includes manual code review, automated testing, architecture analysis, and collaborative Q&A sessions with your team.

Do you provide post-audit support?

+

Yes, we offer ongoing support including fixes review and consultation for implementing security recommendations.
